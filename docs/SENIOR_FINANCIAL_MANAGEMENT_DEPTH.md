# Senior — Financial Management Depth

The senior layer treats financial management as a first-class professional curriculum area.

## Scope

It covers capital preservation, cash flow, budgeting, liquidity, manual operation discipline, position sizing, allocation, concentration, drawdown and recovery math, leverage/margin/collateral, reserves, transaction costs, performance attribution, tax-aware recordkeeping, portfolio capital allocation, stress testing and contingency planning.

## Experience standard

- Baseline: 45+ years of professional-depth context.
- Open-ended: there is no artificial upper ceiling.
- Advanced/expert: competencies are intended for professional-grade reasoning and continuous reassessment.
- Evidence: knowledge remains subject to provenance, validation, counterevidence and current jurisdiction/instrument context.
- Execution: financial-management knowledge never grants trading, transfer, withdrawal or REAL execution authority.

## Manual operation

The curriculum explicitly covers the financial discipline required when the user operates manually: defining available capital, separating reserve from risk capital, sizing exposure, accounting for costs, evaluating drawdown and preserving liquidity before and after an operation.

## Leverage

Leverage is treated separately from point/tick monetary conversion. The senior layer must understand exposure, margin and collateral without multiplying the monetary value of a price movement by leverage a second time.
